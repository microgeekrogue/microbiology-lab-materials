⚠ Common Student Mistakes

  • Confusing objective magnification with total magnification.
  • Using the coarse adjustment knob under high power.
  • Applying immersion oil before switching to the 100× objective.
  • Mixing up the 40× and 100× objectives.
  • Leaving oil residue on microscope lenses.
  • Storing the microscope under high power rather than the 4× objective.
