02 Can I Connect Structure to Function?

Compare the structural clues, major functions, and examples for each biomolecule class.

Biomolecule Key Structural Clues Major Function Examples
Carbohydrates Many hydroxyl groups and ring structures Short-term energy supply Glucose, starch, bread
Lipids Long hydrocarbon chains Energy storage and membranes Butter, oils, triglycerides
Proteins Amino acids joined by peptide bonds Structure and enzymes Hemoglobin, collagen, enzymes
Nucleic Acids Phosphates, sugars, nitrogenous bases Genetic information DNA and RNA

Structural Clues

  • Many repeating –OH groups often indicate carbohydrates.
  • Long carbon chains usually suggest lipids.
  • Amino acid building blocks and peptide bonds point toward proteins.
  • Phosphate groups, sugars, and bases together suggest nucleic acids.

Why Health Professionals Care

  • Glucose supplies energy to cells.
  • Lipids form cell membranes.
  • Proteins function as enzymes and tissue builders.
  • DNA stores genetic information.

03 Biomolecule Spotter Challenge

Practice explaining your identification using evidence from the molecule.

Use Visual Evidence

  1. Identify the biomolecule category.
  2. List two structural clues.
  3. Explain one biological function.
  4. Name a real-world example.
  5. Explain why another biomolecule category is less appropriate.

Example Identification

“This molecule is classified as a carbohydrate because it contains a ring structure and several hydroxyl groups. A carbohydrate provides energy for cells. A lipid would be less likely because long hydrocarbon chains are absent.”

04 Quick Identification Practice

Make your identification before revealing each answer.

Practice 1

A molecule contains many hydroxyl groups and forms a six-membered ring.

Reveal answer

Most likely a carbohydrate.

Practice 2

A molecule contains long hydrocarbon chains and very little oxygen.

Reveal answer

Most likely a lipid.

Practice 3

A molecule is built from amino acids joined by peptide bonds.

Reveal answer

Most likely a protein.

Practice 4

A molecule contains phosphate groups, sugars, and nitrogenous bases.

Reveal answer

Most likely a nucleic acid.

Common Student Mistakes

  • Assuming every ring structure is a carbohydrate.
  • Confusing lipids with hydrocarbons because both contain many carbon-hydrogen bonds.
  • Ignoring functional groups when classifying molecules.
  • Memorizing examples instead of evaluating structure.
  • Identifying a biomolecule solely from a food source.
  • Forgetting that function often supports identification.
