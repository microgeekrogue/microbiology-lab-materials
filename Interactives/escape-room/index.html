🦠 Room 3: Contaminated Culture

Aseptic Technique
Scenario: You notice unexpected colonies on a "pure culture" streak plate, including different sizes and pigments. Decide which actions are appropriate when contamination is suspected.
Contaminated Petri Dish.

🔐 Puzzle: Contamination Decision Grid

Select the two best actions to take when you suspect plate contamination. Type the two letters together (e.g., AB) in alphabetical order for the lock:

  • A – Tape the plate shut, label it as contaminated, and place it in the designated biohazard disposal.
  • B – Open the plate fully on the bench to inspect colony texture with no flame.
  • C – Note in your lab notebook that the culture appears contaminated and inform the instructor.
  • D – Take the plate home to examine later with a magnifying glass.
  • E – Autoclave or disinfect the plate according to lab policy before disposal.

🔐 Puzzle: Exit Order Combination

Choose the best order for these exit tasks. Use the numbers 1–4 in the correct sequence (no spaces) to form the final lock code:

  1. (1) Remove gloves and other disposable PPE and discard into appropriate biohazard or trash containers.
  2. (2) Disinfect bench top thoroughly and allow appropriate contact time.
  3. (3) Wash hands with soap and water for the required duration.
  4. (4) Log out of the lab, collect personal items, and exit.

Hint: Think about when hands are the cleanest relative to touching door handles and personal items.
