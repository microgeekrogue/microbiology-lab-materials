Station 3

🍄 Meet the Fungus: Claviceps purpurea

Click each part of the ergot story to reveal a scientific clue.

🌾 Infects rye
Claviceps purpurea infects rye and related grasses. The fungal structure replaces part of the grain with a hard ergot body called a sclerotium.
⚫ Forms ergots
Ergots are dark purple-black sclerotia. They contain ergot alkaloids, including compounds related to lysergic acid.
☠️ Produces toxins
Ergot alkaloids can cause ergotism. Depending on the toxins and dose, effects may include convulsions, hallucinations, vomiting, burning sensations, and circulation problems.
🌧️ Thrives in wet conditions
Rainfall or high soil moisture supports ergot development. A cold winter followed by a wet spring can increase the chance of contamination in rye crops.
