Microbe Hunters • A bacterial identification investigation

Welcome, aspiring Microbe Detectives!

Are you ready to embark on an exciting journey into the unseen world of microscopic organisms?

Your mission is to unmask the identity of an unknown bacterium using Gram staining, morphology, culture characteristics, hemolysis, fermentation tests, H₂S production, oxidase, and catalase.

Each test is a clue. Each result brings you closer to solving the case.
